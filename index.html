Handa na ba kayo?
Is your family ready?

The Philippines faces more typhoons, floods, earthquakes and fires than almost any country. HandaPH gives you clear, step-by-step guidance so you know what to do before, during and after an emergency.

Three steps to a ready household

  1. Learn your hazards. Read the guide for your area, then check your barangay hazard map. Start at Choose a Hazard.
  2. Get your supplies. Tick off the checklist and pack a go-bag using the kit guide.
  3. Make a plan together. Fill in the family plan, choose meeting points and learn your evacuation route.

For everyone, in every community

Preparedness only works when it includes everyone: persons with disabilities, older adults, children, pregnant and nursing mothers, indigenous communities and families on tight budgets. Our inclusive preparedness guide shows how to adapt your plan, and our community page shows how neighbours and barangays can prepare together.

This site supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 11, target 11.5: significantly reduce the number of people affected by disasters.

About this project

Start with a 25-second lesson

Drop, Cover and Hold On

What to do in the first seconds of an earthquake. About 25 seconds, no sound; the steps appear as large on-screen text. Original animation made for HandaPH (see credits).

Read the transcript (text version)
  1. Drop: get down on your hands and knees right away.
  2. Cover: get under a sturdy table or desk, or cover your head and neck with your arms.
  3. Hold on: hold the table leg and stay put until the shaking stops.
  4. Then move: walk calmly to an open area, away from buildings, posts and glass.
  5. Stay informed: expect aftershocks, check PHIVOLCS advisories and call 911 for emergencies.

In an emergency, call 911. For the latest advice, always follow your barangay, local DRRMO and official government advisories.

HandaPH

Handa means “ready” in Filipino. HandaPH helps Filipino families, schools and communities prepare for typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires and volcanic eruptions. It supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.
