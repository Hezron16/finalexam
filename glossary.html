Glossary

Plain-language meanings of words used on HandaPH and in official advisories.

Aftershock
A smaller earthquake that follows a larger one, sometimes hours or days later.
Barangay
The smallest local government unit in the Philippines and the first responder in a disaster.
BDRRMC
Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committee.
DRRMO
Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office of a city, municipality or province.
Evacuation centre
A safe building, often a school or gym, where people stay during an emergency.
Go-bag
A small bag packed with essentials that you can grab quickly.
Hazard
A natural or human-made event that can cause harm. It becomes a disaster when it affects people who are not prepared.
Intensity
How strongly an earthquake is felt at a place. Magnitude is the size of the earthquake at its source.
Lahar
A fast-moving mixture of volcanic debris and water.
NDRRMC
National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council.
OCD
Office of Civil Defense, which coordinates disaster work.
PAGASA
The Philippine weather agency.
PHIVOLCS
The Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology.
Resilience
The ability to withstand a disaster and recover quickly.
Storm surge
An abnormal rise of sea water pushed ashore by a storm.
TCWS
Tropical Cyclone Wind Signal, PAGASA’s five-level warning system.
Tsunami
A series of large sea waves, usually caused by an undersea earthquake.

HandaPH

Handa means “ready” in Filipino. HandaPH helps Filipino families, schools and communities prepare for typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires and volcanic eruptions. It supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.
