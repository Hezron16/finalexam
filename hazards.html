Choose Your Hazard

Different hazards need different actions. Pick the one that matters most where you live, then follow the guide. Not sure? Check your barangay hazard map or ask your local DRRMO.

Typhoon

About 20 tropical cyclones enter the Philippine Area of Responsibility each year. Learn the wind signals and what to do at each level.

Read the typhoon guide

Flood

Floods can come from heavy rain, river overflow or storm surge. Prepare before the water rises.

Read the flood guide

Earthquake

The Philippines lies on the Pacific Ring of Fire. Know what to do the moment the ground shakes.

Read the earthquake guide

Fire

Most home fires can be prevented. Plan two ways out of every room and practise.

Read the fire guide

Volcano

The country has 24 active volcanoes. Understand alert levels, ashfall and danger zones.

Read the volcano guide

Which agency should I follow?

Official sources for each hazard
HazardAgencyWhat they provide
Typhoon, rain, floodPAGASAWeather bulletins, wind signals, rainfall warnings, flood advisories
Earthquake, tsunami, volcanoPHIVOLCSEarthquake information, tsunami advisories, volcano alert levels
FireBureau of Fire Protection (BFP)Fire response, fire safety inspections, public education
All hazardsNDRRMC and Office of Civil DefenseNational situation reports, coordination and evacuation updates

In an emergency, call 911. For the latest advice, always follow your barangay, local DRRMO and official government advisories.

HandaPH

Handa means “ready” in Filipino. HandaPH helps Filipino families, schools and communities prepare for typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires and volcanic eruptions. It supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.
