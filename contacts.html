Emergency Contacts

Save these numbers in your phone and write them on paper in your go-bag, because phones can run out of battery.

Numbers to know

Emergency numbers
WhoWhen to call
911National emergency hotline for police, fire and medical emergencies.
143Philippine Red Cross hotline for ambulance, first aid and disaster response.
Your barangay hallEvacuation centres, local warnings and relief.
Your city or municipal DRRMOLocal hazard information and emergency response.

Official agencies

Government sources for information
AgencyUse it for
PAGASAWeather forecasts, typhoon bulletins, rainfall and flood warnings
PHIVOLCSEarthquakes, tsunamis and volcano alerts

Philippine Red CrossFirst aid training, ambulance, relief

HandaPH

Handa means “ready” in Filipino. HandaPH helps Filipino families, schools and communities prepare for typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires and volcanic eruptions. It supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.
